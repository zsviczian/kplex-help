# Build and edit

Organize an idea while you are looking at its context. Create notes, connect them and refine their relationships directly from the map.

## What do you want to do?

- **Tidy relationship properties:** [[Manage and clean up ontology|Manage ontology and find its documents]].
- **Connect ideas:** [[Create a relationship|Create a relationship]] — use a role and a property field.
- **Plan a future note:** [[Create notes and placeholders|Create notes and placeholders]].
- **Change a relationship's direction:** [[Drag drop and reclassify|Drag, drop and reclassify]].
- **Understand an edge before editing:** [[Connection details and evidence|Connection details and evidence]].
- **Remove something safely:** [[Unlink and delete|Unlink and delete]].
- **Create drawing notes:** [[Excalidraw integration|Optional Excalidraw integration]].
- **Use the map elsewhere:** [[Export and share a canvas|Export a PNG, linked outline or visit trail]].
- **Work with scripts or assistants:** [[Automation and scripting|Read ontology, relationships and canvas context through the public API]].

**Try this:** In two disposable notes, make a Friend relationship using the `Related` property. Open **Connection details…** to see the source. Do not test deletion on important material.

A full guided path is at [[03 Model and edit relationships|Model and edit relationships]].

## Dates and property entry

- [[Date properties and daily notes|Date properties and daily notes]] — make native Dates point in the intended spatial direction.
- [[Ontology autocomplete|Ontology autocomplete]] — use editor triggers to insert the fields that define relationships.

Try the [[12 Connect dates and daily notes|Dates and daily notes]] discovery route for a complete example.
