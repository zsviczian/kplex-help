# First 5 minutes

Understand the map before changing your own notes. Take a short walk through this help vault: find a note, follow its connections and return to where you started.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Reading relationships.png]]

*The research example keeps a question and its surrounding context together. Open a neighbor to explore it.*

![[Icon - Stopwatch.svg|105]]

1. Install and enable **K-Plex** in Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer, if it is not already enabled.
2. Run **Open K-Plex** from Obsidian's Command Palette or choose the K-Plex ribbon icon.
3. Focus **Search Vault** (default **Alt/Option+/**, or click its field), search for **K-Plex Help**, and select the result. Existing custom or older shortcuts may differ.
4. Single-click a **Child** to make it the new center. Use **Past nodes** or back navigation to return; double-click a file-backed node to open its note in Obsidian.
5. Notice the spatial directions: **Parent above**, **Child below**, **Friend / Previous left**, and **Challenger / Next right**.
6. Right-click a node, gate or connector to discover its actions. On a touch device, long-press instead.
7. Press **Ctrl/Cmd+F** with the graph focused to **Find in Plex**. This is different from whole-vault **Search Vault**.

> [!note] Ready does not mean finished
> Filename search and the current note's Obsidian-known relationships can work while richer indexing and URL discovery continue. There is no need to wait for the full URL inventory to browse this help.

## Try one small discovery

Open [[00 Start Here/Choose a topic|Choose a topic]], select **First steps with K-Plex**, then follow its child notes. You can also go straight to [[01 Core concepts/Relationship roles|Relationship roles]].

[Watch a K-Plex introduction](https://youtu.be/O-7JHDj5d7Y)

> [!warning] Editing is real
> Creating, moving or unlinking explicit graph relationships can change source note properties. Use disposable notes for your first editing exercise; keep your normal vault backups.

## Try the new keyboard and focus controls

Focus the graph with **Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+G**, type `Glossary` to select a visible label, and press Enter to navigate. Open the **K-Plex command palette** with **Alt/Option+.**; use **Alt/Option+?** for keyboard help. For a larger view, try **Expand** for Zen mode (all devices) and **Maximize** for fullscreen (desktop).

Follow [[10 Discover by topic/11 Work without the mouse|Work without the mouse]] or [[10 Discover by topic/13 Present and concentrate|Present and concentrate]] for a guided exercise.
