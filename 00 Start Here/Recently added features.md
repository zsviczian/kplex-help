# Find a useful improvement

Returning to K-Plex and wondering what is worth trying? Start with the task you want to make easier, then open the relevant guide.

## Take the map into your next task

Export a PNG for a presentation, copy a linked Markdown outline into your writing, or capture your visit trail. [[Export and share a canvas|Choose an export]], then try [[Bring a connected outline into your writing|writing from a connected outline]].

## Give a script or assistant useful context

Read your ontology, find indexed notes, inspect relationship evidence or describe a specific open canvas through the public API. Use it inside Obsidian or through Obsidian CLI. [[Automation and scripting|Start with automation and scripting]], including examples and a skill file for local assistants.

## Find and revisit ideas

Pin Today, This week or This month to return to the current periodic note, and reorder pins by dragging or their Move left/right actions. A missing periodic note opens a creation confirmation; Today can use your core Daily Notes template. Search notes, folders and hierarchical tags, use Footprints to retrace a canvas's visit trail, or let Wander bring an old idea back into view. [[Navigate and explore|Choose a navigation guide]].

## Connect and organize your work

Drag supported nodes between canvases, bring links into a draft, choose a new note's destination, and classify notes with tags or a Note type. Manage paired ontology fields and inspect their document uses before cleaning them up. [[Build and edit|Choose an editing workflow]].

Create notes or subfolders from a folder's Child gate, import external files or folders in one batch, or use a node's Move action to change its real location. Creation and linking remember independent After saving choices. Flip an eligible relationship property's owner after reviewing a before-and-after preview.

Add descriptions to explain when to use each ontology field, including independent wording for inverse pairs. Search also matches those descriptions; local assistants can inspect field usage and prepare reviewed configuration changes. Choose a drawing template when creating a related drawing, a folder child or a real file for a placeholder; the last successful choice is remembered.

## Choose a perspective for the question

Compass keeps the familiar directional neighborhood. Mind Map keeps a stable hierarchy root while you unfold branches. Facets compares direct neighbors through categories you define using properties, tags, note types or rules. [[Canvas views|Compare the three views]], then [[Facets|make a useful workflow visible]].

Each Facets preset can place the active note in its matching category, hide gates, or do both. Compass can flip its East–West axis without changing relationship meaning.

## Reveal the detail you need

Group a busy Compass neighborhood by note type, expand another relationship layer, open headings inside a long note, or filter and style the current canvas with reusable Graph Lenses. [[Filter and focus|Choose how to focus]].

## Give reading and writing more room

Read beside the map in a Sidecar or edit a supported note on the canvas. Alt/Option-select the Sidecar controls to give the document or canvas the available space in one action. Framing adapts when the Sidecar opens or closes and respects Autozoom. [[Personalize and workspace|Tune your workspace]].

Open files directly from Obsidian's **K-Plex** context submenu. Use **View** in the canvas context menu to switch perspectives, or enable **Trackpad navigation** to scroll to pan and pinch to zoom. Node editors keep their recovery controls reachable, and File properties follows the open editor.

## Get back to work while preparation continues

Choose startup and Eager/On-demand behavior separately for desktop, tablet and phone. Recent indexing improvements reuse valid work during edits and warm starts; gate counters remain stable through ordinary autosaves. Compatible cached relationships remain useful while Sync changes are validated. Local work takes priority, and recovery avoids repeated reads. Current-neighborhood readiness and wider discovery remain separate. [[Indexing and troubleshooting|Understand loading and recovery]].

## Put it to work

Try [[Never start a project with a blank page|starting a project from existing knowledge]], [[Garden your vault|gardening one note at a time]], or [[Research and learning|turning research into a draft]]. Read [[Release 0.4.2|what changed in 0.4.2]], or use the release archive for earlier history.
