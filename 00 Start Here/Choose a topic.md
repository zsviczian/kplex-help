# Choose a topic

Looking for one useful technique rather than a whole manual? Choose the kind of work you want to do, then follow a short route through the help.

## Choose your goal

- [[10 Discover by topic/Find and revisit knowledge|Find and revisit knowledge]]: first steps, searching, websites and keyboard navigation.
- [[10 Discover by topic/Connect and develop ideas|Connect and develop ideas]]: relationships, research, projects, sequences and dates.
- [[10 Discover by topic/Make the workspace work for you|Make the workspace work for you]]: filtering, appearance, presentation and troubleshooting.

Each group contains short paths through existing guides. You can follow links in the note or center the group in K-Plex and open a Child. You do not need to finish one path before trying another.

Prefer a practical result? [[07 Use cases/Use cases|Choose an everyday workflow]] such as preparing an article or recovering meeting context. Need only a definition? Use the [[00 Start Here/Glossary|glossary]].
