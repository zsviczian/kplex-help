# Read relationships with Compass

Understand an idea by looking at its context, detail, companions and objections. Compass gives those relationships familiar positions around the current note.

## Try the directional map

1. Center [[09 Showcase examples/Example - Research question|the research question]].
2. Look above for broader context, below for detail, left for companions or previous steps, and right for challenges or next steps.
3. Select a neighbor to make it the new center and explore its context.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Reading relationships.png]]

*The research question is surrounded by its real relationships. Compass keeps the same directional vocabulary as you move.*

## Open the detail you need

A gate tells you whether relationships exist in that direction. Expand a busy neighborhood one level further, group related notes by note type, or filter the current canvas. Use One-sided layout for a narrow space. These choices change what you see, while your recorded relationships retain their meaning.

Eligible Markdown notes can open in a native node editor in their place on the canvas. There is one node editor at a time; opening another saves and closes the first. A failed save keeps it available for recovery. A Sidecar gives you a separate document pane beside the canvas. Use **Settings → K-Plex → Views → Compass** for Compass arrangement, density and area tint.
