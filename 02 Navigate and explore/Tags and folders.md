# Browse by tag or folder

Know a topic or location, but not the name of the note you need? Start from a tag or folder and follow its structure into the relevant files.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Search folders.png]]

*Search matches parts of folder names and paths. Folder icons distinguish locations from notes in the same result list.*

## Choose your way in

- [[02 Navigate and explore/Explore hierarchical tags|Explore a topic through its tags]]: see a broad tag, its narrower child tags and the notes that use them.
- [[02 Navigate and explore/Explore folders|Explore a folder and its files]]: move through the vault's existing file structure.

Both are available in [[02 Navigate and explore/Vault Search vs Find in this Plex|Search Vault]]. Their result icons help distinguish a tag or folder from a note with a similar name. These views use the tags and folders you already have; you do not need to build a parallel hierarchy of notes.
