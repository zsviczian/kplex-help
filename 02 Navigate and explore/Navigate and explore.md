Use this section when you need to **go somewhere**, **find something already visible**, or **keep context while moving**. Those are different jobs.

## Pick your task

- **Move around the map:** [[02 Navigate and explore/Navigating the Plex|Navigating the Plex]] — click, open, pan, zoom and use context menus.
- **Search across notes and URLs:** [[02 Navigate and explore/Vault Search vs Find in this Plex|Search Vault vs Find in this Plex]] — one searches the vault; the other highlights the current Plex.
- **Go back or keep a landmark:** [[02 Navigate and explore/Past nodes and pins|Past nodes and pins]].
- **Read a file or website:** [[02 Navigate and explore/Editor node and web pages|Editor node and web pages]].
- **Explore on a tablet/phone:** [[02 Navigate and explore/Mobile and touch|Mobile and touch]].

**Try this:** Use **Alt/Option+/** (or your configured Search Vault key) to navigate to a different note, then **Ctrl/Cmd+F** to locate one of its visible neighbors. Find does not change the center.

Follow [[10 Discover by topic/02 Find and navigate knowledge|Find and navigate knowledge]] for a complete route.
