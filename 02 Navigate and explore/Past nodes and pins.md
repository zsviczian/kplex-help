# Past nodes and pins

Return to where you were, or keep a project within easy reach. Past nodes remembers recent navigation; pins keep the landmarks you choose.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Pins.png]]

*Pins keep chosen landmarks above the graph.*

![[Assets/Screenshot - Past nodes.png]]

*Past nodes keeps recent destinations below the graph, with icons for the target type.*

Pins are useful for anchors such as a project home, a research question, a dashboard note, or this help home. History is useful for retracing exploration.

Past nodes and pins use your node styles, including note-type and tag colors, borders, icons and prefixes. They keep compact list sizes; the current destination has a separate accent outline.

To scroll Past nodes with a mouse, hold the primary button and drag left or right. A short click still opens the chosen note. Touch swiping, trackpad scrolling and keyboard activation remain available.

You can also create relationships from these surfaces. Drag a relationship gate onto a history entry or pinned note to connect using that gate's relationship type. Dragging a node body onto a history entry or pin lets you choose the relationship type.

This help vault starts with a few useful pins, including [[00 Start Here/K-Plex Help|K-Plex Help]] and [[01 Core concepts/Relationship roles|Relationship roles]].

**Keyboard routes:** **Backspace** goes back and **Shift+Backspace** goes forward when no type-to-select phrase is active. **Alt/Option+B** opens pins, **Alt/Option+H** opens the full history picker, and **Alt/Option+P** toggles the current selection/center pin. These defaults can be changed in [[05 Personalize and workspace/Actions and shortcuts|Actions and shortcuts]].

## Want every visit in just this canvas?

[[02 Navigate and explore/Footprints and breadcrumbs|Footprints (session breadcrumbs)]] records the complete visit sequence of **one canvas**, newest first, including repeated visits. Use the footprints icon next to Find in canvas, rather than the shared Past nodes bar at the bottom. Footprints is **not persisted**: it survives moving that canvas between windows, but clears when the canvas closes or Obsidian restarts.

Selecting an earlier Footprints visit appends another visit. **Start here** resets only that canvas's trail, while **Copy as Markdown** exports the whole list for pasting into a note. Neither action clears Past nodes or pins. For a practical exercise, try [[07 Use cases/Rediscover and remember your notes]].
