K-Plex keeps a **Past nodes** history for backward/forward navigation. This history changes as you move. **Pins** are separate: they are stable shortcuts you choose to keep available.

Pins are useful for anchors such as a project home, a research question, a dashboard note, or this help home. History is useful for retracing exploration.

You can also create relationships from these surfaces. Drag a relationship gate onto a history entry or pinned note to connect using that gate's relationship type. Dragging a node body onto a history entry or pin lets you choose the relationship type.

This help vault preconfigures a few pins in `data.json`, including [[00 Start Here/K-Plex Help|K-Plex Help]] and [[01 Core concepts/Relationship roles|Relationship roles]].

**Keyboard routes:** **Backspace** goes back and **Shift+Backspace** goes forward when no type-to-select phrase is active. **Alt/Option+B** opens pins, **Alt/Option+H** opens the full history picker, and **Alt/Option+P** toggles the current selection/center pin. These defaults can be changed in [[05 Personalize and workspace/Actions and shortcuts|Actions and shortcuts]].
