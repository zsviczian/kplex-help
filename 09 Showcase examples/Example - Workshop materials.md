# Gather workshop material

Use a useful starting set of workshop examples. Keep the source ideas connected to one preparation note so you can return to them while building your session.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Expanded neighbors.png]]

*The workshop plan stays central while the material branch reveals its two source notes one level further down.*

## Try it

The visual note gives you a recognizable image; the research example keeps a question and its context close. In your own workshop, replace these with examples, references and exercises that serve your audience.

When the workshop is over, follow Next to record what you would keep or change. These are ordinary notes and links, so you can also work with them in Obsidian's editor.
