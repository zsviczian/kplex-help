# Where does the workshop material stand?

Prepare a workshop without losing track of the material you already have. This board groups connected examples by what you want to do with them next.

## Try it

1. Center this note and choose **Facets → Workshop stage** in **Views and lenses**.
2. Look across **Collect → Shape → Share**. The materials and research question help you collect; the visual note and sectioned note help you shape; the review and visual-ideas workflow help you share what you learned.
3. Open a connected note's properties and inspect **Workshop stage**. The category comes from this exact property, regardless of its relationship role.

The areas deliberately have different widths: compare a wider collection with a narrower shaping list. The arrows describe a suggested workflow; they do not create additional links.

## Make it yours

Use a project note as the focus and connect the work you need. Name stages that help you decide what to do next. This is an example board, not a claim that a workshop took place. To practice category assignment, work on your own copy and review the property preview before confirming.
