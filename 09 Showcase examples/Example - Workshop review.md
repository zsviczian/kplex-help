# Keep a lesson from the workshop

Want the next workshop to benefit from this one? Keep a review beside the plan so observations do not disappear into an unrelated daily note.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Expanded neighbors.png]]

*The workshop plan stays central while the material branch reveals its two source notes one level further down.*

## Try it in your own project

Write one thing to keep and one thing to change. Link the relevant material rather than copying it into the review. Follow Previous to revisit preparation or Parent to return to the plan. The materials note declares Next; the paired Previous label gives you the return route without a second property here.

This example does not claim a workshop took place. It shows a reusable place for your own observations after an activity.
