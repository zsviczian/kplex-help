# Plan a short workshop

Reuse your notes when preparing a workshop. Start with a plan, connect the material you need and leave a place to capture what you learn afterward.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Expanded neighbors.png]]

*The workshop plan stays central while the material branch reveals its two source notes one level further down.*

## Try the overview

Center this plan in K-Plex and enable **Expanded view: show each node's children**. The materials note has two source notes beneath it. You can look one step into that branch without leaving the workshop overview.

Each stage names this plan in its Parent property, so it appears as a Child without a second list here. Open the materials note when you want to inspect its context in detail. Follow Next from there to the review. In your own project, the connections can point to notes you already own; you do not need to copy them into a new folder.
