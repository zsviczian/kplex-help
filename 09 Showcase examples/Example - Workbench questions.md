# What fits the time and attention you have?

Choose a useful next step when you have either a brief gap or a longer session. Two ordinary properties make the choices visible without changing the notes’ relationships.

## Try it

1. Center this note and choose **Facets → Workbench questions**.
2. Compare **Read briefly**, **Read deeply**, **Try something small** and **Set aside practice time**.
3. Inspect a note's `learningMode` and `sessionSize` properties. They combine `read` or `practice` with `short` or `longer`.
4. Open the preset editor and select **Category rule** to inspect the conditions. Each quadrant requires both facts to match.

For example, the Read briefly rule is `note.learningMode == "read" and note.sessionSize == "short"`. The categories are separate combinations, so a known note fits one place. The quadrant arrangement keeps all four choices available without claiming one is always better.

## Make it yours

Choose two simple distinctions that help you select work: effort and urgency, reading and writing, or confidence and readiness. Record the facts rather than asking a rule to guess them from prose. This is a local view of connected notes, not a timed task manager.
