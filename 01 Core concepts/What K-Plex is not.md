# What K-Plex is not

Trying to decide whether K-Plex is the right tool for a task? Use it to explore a meaningful neighborhood; use a vault-wide search or query when you need a complete inventory.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Reading relationships.png]]

*The research example keeps a question and its surrounding context together. Open a neighbor to explore it.*

Its core working surface is the **current neighborhood around one center**. Graph Lenses evaluate the currently visible canvas; they do not run a deep arbitrary graph query over the entire vault. Section expansion is temporary; it does not permanently turn every heading into a global node. On-demand indexing can also mean that background-derived information arrives after the immediately useful local graph.

These boundaries are strengths when your goal is navigation: they keep the graph readable, make spatial meaning predictable, and let foreground actions receive priority over full-vault maintenance.

If you need a whole-vault inventory, combine K-Plex with Obsidian search, Bases, Dataview, or other tools built for global queries.
