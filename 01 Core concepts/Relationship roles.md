# Relationship roles

Tell a broader idea from a detail, a supporting note from an objection, or an earlier step from the next one. Relationship roles give those connections recognizable places on the map.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Reading relationships.png]]

*The research example keeps a question and its surrounding context together. Open a neighbor to explore it.*

![[Idea Compass]]

| Role | Region | Useful for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Parent** | Above | Broader concept, container, project goal |
| **Child** | Below | Part, example, deliverable, subtopic |
| **Friend** | Left | Related, supporting or similar idea |
| **Challenger** | Right | Objection, risk, alternative, competing interpretation |
| **Previous** | Left | Earlier step, preceding version or event |
| **Next** | Right | Following step, next action or event |

**Ontology fields are configurable.** This help vault uses `Guide` as Parent, `Explains` as Child, `Related` as Friend, and `Watch out` as Challenger. Your vault can use domain language such as `Risk` or `Example` instead, provided the mapping is configured.

**Try this:** Center [[01 Core concepts/Ontology and relationship fields|Ontology and relationship fields]] and inspect the linked notes to its left and right. Right-click a connector to open **Connection details…** and examine its source. Use a Challenger for a question or objection worth considering.
