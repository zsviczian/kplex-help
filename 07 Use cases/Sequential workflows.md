**Outcome:** You can follow a repeatable process, learning path or project history while keeping the instructions, evidence and exceptions for each step close at hand.

## Try it: make a process reusable

Choose a process you repeat, such as preparing a workshop or publishing an article. Create a parent note for the whole process and notes for its meaningful stages: preparation, delivery and review, for example.

Connect each stage to the process with Parent/Child relationships. Add explicit Previous/Next connections between the stages to express their order. From a stage, connect the relevant checklist, template, source material or completed example. Use a Challenger for a recurring failure mode or an alternative approach worth considering.

Walk through the sequence as you do the work. At the end, record a lesson in the relevant stage or link a separate retrospective. The next run starts with both instructions and experience, not just an empty checklist.

**Done when:** Someone can find the next step, understand why it exists and reach the material needed to complete it.

## Keep order separate from belonging

Parent/Child answers “Where does this belong?” Previous/Next answers “What comes before or after this?” The same note can answer both questions without forcing sequence into a hierarchy.

Sequential roles share the lateral regions with Friends and Challengers in the standard layout. Their relationship labels retain the distinction. They are useful for procedures, reading sequences, journal chains, document versions and release histories—not only project tasks.

**Dates do not automatically mean Previous/Next.** Use a Date relationship that genuinely represents your intended meaning; see [[03 Build and edit/Date properties and daily notes]]. K-Plex does not execute the procedure, enforce completion order or calculate a timeline.
