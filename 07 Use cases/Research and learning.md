# Research and learning

Have plenty of reading notes but no clear argument yet? Build an article around a question, connect its claims to sources and keep counter-evidence visible.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Read beside the map.png]]

*A normal Markdown pane sits beside the Plex so you can read or write while keeping the note's context visible.*

**Outcome:** A collection of reading notes becomes an argument or article outline whose claims remain connected to their sources.

## Try it: answer a question rather than collect more material

Create a note for the question your article will answer. Connect a few relevant source notes, then explore their neighbors for repeated concepts, useful examples and disagreements. With the article as the center, sources can be Parents when the relationship means “this work grows from that material.”

Create short notes for the claims you intend to make. Connect each claim to its evidence and add a Challenger when a source limits or contradicts it. Group the claims into a developing outline rather than treating the order in which you found sources as the order of your argument.

Keep the draft open in a normal Obsidian editor or a fixed reference tab. Add links to the notes that support each section and write the narrative in your own words. A relationship lens can focus on an explicitly configured field, but it does not automatically extract an entire argument from the vault.

**Done when:** You have a draft structure, supporting sources for its main claims and at least one objection you have addressed or left visibly unresolved.

## Choose relationships for the question you are asking

A research collection can instead use Parents for disciplines or umbrella questions and Children for papers, experiments and subtopics. Friends hold related concepts, methods and supporting evidence; Challengers hold competing theories or counter-evidence. Previous/Next can express an intentional reading sequence.

These are different perspectives, not competing rules about where every paper must go. Configure custom relationship fields deliberately and inspect **Connection details…** when a connection is unclear.

## From graph to draft

Drag a file-backed note, URL or placeholder node into a Markdown editor to insert its link. You still write the synthesis yourself; K-Plex does not export an automatically ordered article. See [[03 Build and edit/Drag drop and reclassify]].

Use [[05 Personalize and workspace/Note-tab synchronization]] to control whether the open document follows navigation. Each Plex owns its document-sync target and Sidecar association, so two views can follow different working notes.
