A research topic can use hierarchy and lateral context at the same time:

- **Parents** — disciplines, research questions or umbrella concepts.
- **Children** — papers, experiments, subtopics or examples.
- **Friends** — related concepts, methods or supporting evidence.
- **Challengers** — competing theories, counter-evidence or unresolved objections.
- **Previous / Next** — a reading or learning sequence.

This model is especially useful when a note's meaning depends on both *where it belongs* and *what it is in tension with*. Graph Lenses can then temporarily emphasize papers with a tag, one ontology field, or one kind of evidence.
