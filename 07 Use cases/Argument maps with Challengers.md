# Argument maps with Challengers

Need to make a decision you can explain and revisit? Put the proposal beside its evidence, objections and assumptions so you can see what would change your mind.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Reading relationships.png]]

*The research example includes a scope caveat as a Challenger. In your own decision, use that direction for a genuine objection or condition.*

**Outcome:** A proposal becomes a better-grounded decision, with its assumptions, objections and conditions for reconsideration visible.

## Try it: examine a decision you are about to make

Write the proposal as a note—for example, “Replace our current planning tool.” Make it the center of K-Plex and use the Idea Compass to investigate it.

Look north for the goal and assumptions behind the proposal. Look south for consequences, work and outcomes it could create. Put supporting evidence and related experience among Friends. Add objections, alternatives and risks as Challengers rather than burying them at the end of the proposal.

Open the strongest objection and explore its own context. What evidence would settle it? Does it expose a genuine weakness, a different goal, or a cost you are willing to accept? Return to the proposal and revise it, narrow its scope, or record why you are not proceeding.

**Done when:** The decision note explains what you chose, why, which uncertainty remains and what new evidence would make you reconsider.

## Challengers are more than a list of cons

The Challenger region can hold:

- [Counter-arguments](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/counterargument) and [disconfirming evidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_bias_mitigation).
- [Alternative designs](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/17/15/7142) and [risks or failure modes](http://homepages.se.edu/cvonbergen/files/2013/01/Performing-a-Project-Premortem.pdf).
- [Trade-offs that cannot be optimized away](https://nissenbaum.tech.cornell.edu/papers/Rethinking%20Optimization:%20A%20Systems-Based%20Approach%20to%20Social%20Externalities.pdf).

Friends and Challengers remain distinct relationships. You can configure `Supports` as Friend and `Opposes` as Challenger, then use connector styles or Graph Lenses to emphasize those fields. The map makes your reasoning inspectable; it does not determine whether an argument is true or assign evidence quality automatically.
