# Filter and focus

Is the map too busy for the question you are working on? Filter distractions, highlight a useful subset or open just the detail you need.

![[Image - Filters.png]]

## Choose a tool

- **Quick Filter:** [[Quick Filter|Temporary keyword, tag or type filters]].
- **Graph Lenses:** [[Graph Lenses|Reusable note, relationship and evidence filters or styling]].
- **Keep layout vs Reflow:** [[Keep layout vs Reflow|Preserve positions or compact the result]].
- **Sections:** [[Expand a note into sections|Expand a long Markdown note by headings]].
- **Another layer:** [[Expanded relationship view|Expand children beneath first-level nodes]].
- **A compact overview:** [[Group nodes by note type|Group related notes by type, then unfold the group you need]].

> [!important] The lens boundary
> [[Graph Lenses are local|Graph Lenses are local]]: they evaluate the currently visible neighborhood. They do not search every disconnected note in your vault.

**Try this:** Open **Views and lenses**, filter for `kplex-help/how-to`, then compare Keep layout and Reflow. Use [[06 Reduce clutter and reveal detail|Reduce clutter and reveal detail]] for the full route.
