# Quick Filter

Need a momentary view of just the relevant notes? Use Quick Filter to narrow the current map by keyword, tag or note type.

![[Screenshot - Attachment visibility.png]]

*Desktop: independent attachment switches appear above Quick lens and saved lenses in the same Views and lenses panel.*

## Narrow this map for a moment

1. Select the funnel button, **Views and lenses**.
2. Enter a keyword, choose a tag or select a note type in Quick Filter.
3. Review the remaining nodes. Clear the filter to bring the rest back.

Keyword matching includes titles, paths, aliases and relationship descriptions. Try `kplex-help/how-to` as a tag in this vault to focus on practical guides.

Choose [[Keep layout vs Reflow|Keep layout or Reflow]] depending on whether you want stable positions or a compact result. The panel can be dragged aside; close and reopen it to return it beside the funnel.

If you want to reuse the same perspective, save a [[Graph Lenses|Graph Lens]] instead. Filters change the view, not your notes.

## Show the kinds of material you need

The Visibility controls appear in this order: **Markdown**, **Tags**, **Folders**, **Web links**, **Placeholders**, **Documents**, **Images**, **Audio & video**, **Other attachments**, **Inferred**, **Siblings**, **Cross-links**.

The attachment switches are independent. Documents includes PDF, EPUB, office and text formats; Images includes common image formats; Audio & video includes sound and movie files; unclassified extensions appear under Other attachments. Existing all-attachment preferences carry over. These switches change visibility, not files or relationships.

The panel stays within its owning canvas, including a narrow phone sidebar or wider tablet sidebar. Resize or scroll the panel without losing its close control.
