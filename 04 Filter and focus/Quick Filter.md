Open **Filters and lenses** with the funnel button. Quick Filter supports temporary filtering by:

- keyword;
- tag;
- note type.

Keyword matching includes title, path, alias and relationship definition. This is ideal when you know you only need a momentary reduction of the current Plex.

The filter panel is draggable. Close and reopen it to return it beside the funnel. If your filter is something you expect to reuse or if it needs relationship/evidence semantics, graduate it into a [[04 Filter and focus/Graph Lenses|Graph Lens]].
