# Group nodes by note type

A project or topic can have more related notes than you want to inspect at once. Group them by note type to see the kinds of material around it, then open just the group you need.

## Try it

1. Center a note connected to several notes of the same type, such as sources, ideas or tasks.
2. Select **Group nodes by note type**, the group icon beside **Expanded view** in the toolbar.
3. Look for a group named after the note type. Its small number outside the bottom-right corner shows how many items it contains.
4. Select a group to unfold its members in place. Your central note stays where it is.
5. Navigate to another note to begin with its relations grouped again, or turn the toggle off to unfold every remaining group.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Grouped workshop relations.png]]

*A desktop workshop example groups three sources, three ideas, two tasks, two attachments and two placeholder notes. Room and equipment stays individual because it is the only untyped note. The numbers sit outside the groups.*

## What belongs together?

Markdown notes use the configured **Node style property**, which defaults to `Note type`. Notes without a value appear together as **Notes**. The group takes its name and appearance from its note type; assigning types and choosing their colors is explained in [[05 Personalize and workspace/Node styles from help tags|node styling]].

Other items have their own groups: **Attachments**, **Links**, **Placeholder notes**, **Tags** and **Folders**. These use the corresponding kind's appearance. A type or kind with only one item stays as an individual node.

Grouping happens separately in each relationship area: Parents and Children remain separate, for example. In One-sided layout, the shared left area groups Friends/Previous together with Challengers/Next.

If everything appears under **Notes**, check the notes' `Note type` values and your configured Node style property. The help vault's style tags give notes colors, but tags alone do not assign the note types used for grouping.

## Keep control of the detail

Unfold one group while leaving the rest compact. The grouping toggle stays on as you navigate, but each newly centered note starts with its groups folded. Unfolding a group in one Plex does not unfold the same group in another Plex.

![[Assets/Screenshot - Unfolded source group.png]]

*Selecting Source reveals its three notes without leaving Workshop plan. The other groups stay compact.*

Groups summarize the items admitted to the current view. Filters, visibility choices and area limits still apply, so a group's number is not a whole-vault total. Grouping changes the view without editing note properties or relationships.

For a different question, use [[04 Filter and focus/Quick Filter|Quick Filter]] to narrow what is shown, or [[04 Filter and focus/Expanded relationship view|Expanded view]] to look one level beyond a neighboring note.
