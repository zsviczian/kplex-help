# Make your own workflow visible with Facets

Bring a question of your own to your connected notes: what is ready to use, which sources need attention, or where should you spend the next ten minutes? Facets lets you define useful categories and arrange the current note’s neighbors around that question.

You choose the categories, the source that classifies notes and the arrangement. A preset saves those choices for reuse. It can describe your project stages, reading priorities or any other distinction already present in your notes; you do not need to adopt somebody else's system.

Fresh K-Plex starts with **Wu Xing** and **Local map roles**. ARC, PARA, GTD horizons, eACCESS, Sensemaking and Publishing pipeline are [optional JSON downloads](https://github.com/zsviczian/kplex/tree/0a1365e820d343c2cf8708c66eeea8417f77cbc7/presets/facets), rather than built-in choices. Import only what you use, or define a workflow of your own. Saved definitions remain available when the built-in set changes.

## Start with a question you actually have

1. Center a note with useful direct connections.
2. In **Views and lenses**, choose **Facets**, then a **Preset**.
3. Compare the categories and inspect anything **Unassigned** or **Conflicting**.
4. Open the preset's help beside its selector, or use its settings control to inspect how it works.

Facets reorganizes the current note and its direct relationships. It does not collect every matching note across your vault. Create meaningful connections first, or use Obsidian search or Bases to find notes to connect.

## Learn from four working examples

This Help vault keeps four presets with different purposes and layouts. They are examples to learn from, not a required workflow or a catalog you must keep. Each has an example note whose connected neighborhood supplies meaningful data.

| Help preset | The question it answers | Classification and arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workshop stage** | Where does this workshop material stand? | A property, in a horizontal flow |
| **Read by topic** | Which kind of help do I need? | Tags, around the center |
| **Learning route** | Should I understand, try or apply this? | Note types, in a vertical sequence |
| **Workbench questions** | What fits the time and attention I have? | Two-property category rules, in four quadrants |

## Build something useful for your notes

[[04 Filter and focus/Define a Facets workflow|Define a workflow]] from three to seven categories. Rename labels, choose colors and supply the exact values or category rules that make sense in your vault. A label can be friendly while its recognized property values remain unchanged.

[[04 Filter and focus/Facets layouts and classification|Choose an arrangement and understand classification]] before treating an empty category as missing work. Property, tag, note-type and rule sources are separate choices; Facets does not silently try another source when a value is missing.

When a workflow is useful, [[04 Filter and focus/Share presets and lenses|share its preset]] with another vault. You choose any companion lenses explicitly; referenced palette colors travel with the definition.
