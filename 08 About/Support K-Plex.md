K-Plex is developed independently. If it is useful to you and you want to support continued development, you can contribute through Ko-fi:

**[Support K-Plex on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/zsolt)**

You can also support the project by joining the **Sketch Your Mind Community**, sharing feedback, testing releases, helping other users, and contributing documentation or code:

**[Join the Sketch Your Mind Community](https://community.sketch-your-mind.com)**

The strongest support is sustained use plus concrete feedback: describe the goal you were trying to achieve, what happened, and how the workflow could be improved.

## The broader visual-thinking ecosystem

You can also support learning and discussion by sharing your experiences in the **[Sketch Your Mind Community](https://community.sketch-your-mind.com/)** and exploring **[Sketch Your Mind — the book](https://community.sketch-your-mind.com/sym)**.
