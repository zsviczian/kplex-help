*Sketch Your Mind* explores visual thinking and how to make ideas easier to understand, connect and remember. Its focus on thinking through relationships complements K-Plex's spatial navigation of an Obsidian vault.

![Cover of Sketch Your Mind](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zsviczian/sketch-your-mind/main/images/cover.jpg)

**[Explore Sketch Your Mind](https://community.sketch-your-mind.com/sym)**

The image is embedded from the [official book-cover file on GitHub](https://github.com/zsviczian/sketch-your-mind/blob/main/images/cover.jpg). It requires an internet connection to display in Obsidian; the book cover is not an offline asset of this help vault.

## Related work

K-Plex is an independent Obsidian plugin for navigating connected knowledge. [[08 About/ExcaliBrain origins|ExcaliBrain]] helped establish its spatial navigation model; the [[08 About/Community GitHub and contributing|community]] is a place to share techniques, ask questions and compare visual thinking workflows.

For ways to support the software project, visit [[08 About/Support K-Plex|Support K-Plex]].
