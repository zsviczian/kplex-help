# A day connected to its notes

Find the notes associated with a day without maintaining a second list of links. This example daily note is the target of the Date property on [[09 Showcase examples/Example - Date and a daily note|the meeting example]].

Center this daily note in K-Plex to see the example below it when the default Date role is Parent. The meeting's ordinary Parent relationship still connects it to its broader guide.

This is a demonstration date, not a record of an actual meeting. In your vault, the daily note can hold observations and links from that day while individual notes keep their own project context.
