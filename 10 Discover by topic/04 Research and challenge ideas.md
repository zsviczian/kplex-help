# Research and challenge ideas

Explore a research question with supporting and opposing context. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

You read, learn, compare explanations, or build an argument.

## Follow the trail

Follow the links below, or center this path in K-Plex and explore its Children.

1. **[[Research and learning|Research and learning]]** — Use roles as a research map: broad questions, evidence and methods.
2. **[[Example - Research question|Example - Research question]]** — See a literal Parent property working in the graph.
3. **[[Argument maps with Challengers|Argument maps with Challengers]]** — Keep counterarguments visibly distinct from support.
4. **[[Relationship sources and inference|Relationship sources and inference]]** — Understand why a link is explicit or inferred.
5. **[[Graph Lenses|Graph Lenses]]** — Highlight the evidence or ontology you want to inspect.

## Try it in your own vault

Create a test research question with one Parent, two supporting Friends, and one Challenger. Center each note to compare its context.

## Try another small step

Select an example note, open the K-Plex palette, and search for **Connection details**. Use the available action only when the required connection context exists.

## Keep your evidence traceable

Open [[Connection details and evidence|Connection details]] on a supported/contested edge and compare source occurrences. When one frontmatter source is wrong, [[Remove one relationship source|remove only that source]] rather than discarding the complete relationship.
