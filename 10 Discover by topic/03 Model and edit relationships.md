# Model and edit relationships

Give links meaning and make safe edits from the graph. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

You want your ideas to have explicit Parent, Child, Friend or Challenger connections.

## Follow the trail

Follow the links below, or center this path in K-Plex and explore its Children.

1. **[[Ontology and relationship fields|Ontology and relationship fields]]** — Understand how your property names map to spatial roles.
2. **[[Create a relationship|Create a relationship]]** — Connect existing notes or create a new endpoint.
3. **[[Create notes and placeholders|Create notes and placeholders]]** — Create a place for an idea before the file exists.
4. **[[Drag drop and reclassify|Drag drop and reclassify]]** — Reclassify a connection by direct manipulation.
5. **[[Connection details and evidence|Connection details and evidence]]** — Check the stored sources before changing an edge.
6. **[[Unlink and delete|Unlink and delete]]** — Understand source-aware unlinking and the delete boundary.

In disposable notes, add a `Related` property to make a Friend. Right-click its connector, open **Connection details…**, and inspect which source created the relationship.
