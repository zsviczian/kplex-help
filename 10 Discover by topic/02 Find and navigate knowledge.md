# Find and navigate knowledge

Find a note, follow its context, and avoid losing your place. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

You know what you want to find but not where it lives in your vault.

## Follow the trail

Follow the links below, or center this path in K-Plex and explore its Children.

1. **[[Vault Search vs Find in this Plex|Vault Search vs Find in this canvas]]** — Choose between Search Vault and Find in this canvas.
2. **[[Navigating the Plex|Navigating the canvas]]** — Center results, pan, zoom, and use context menus.
3. **[[Past nodes and pins|Past nodes and pins]]** — Use history for backtracking and pins as stable anchors.
4. **[[Sidecar|Sidecar]]** — Keep note content alongside the map.
5. **[[Note-tab synchronization|Note-tab synchronization]]** — Choose when note tabs and K-Plex should follow each other.

## More ways to reach your goal

- [[Keyboard navigation|Keyboard navigation]] — select and center nodes using the arrow keys
- [[Type to select visible nodes|Type to select visible nodes]] — choose a label within the current canvas

## Try it in your own vault

Search for **Connection details and evidence** with **Alt/Option+/** (or your customized search shortcut), pin it, navigate elsewhere, then return using the pin or Past nodes.

## Try another small step

From a focused graph, type `Glossary` to select a displayed match, then use Tab to find the next one. See [[11 Work without the mouse|Work without the mouse]].
