# Connect dates and daily notes

Make a Date property a meaningful relationship to a daily note, without rewriting your date values. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

## Follow the trail

1. [[Date properties and daily notes|Date properties and daily notes]] — configure a default Date role and understand its limits.
2. [[Ontology and relationship fields|Ontology and relationship fields]] — learn how a named field overrides the fallback.
3. [[Meeting notes and daily notes|Meeting notes and daily notes]] — choose a useful relationship meaning for dates.
4. [[Example - Date and a daily note|Example - Date and a daily note]] — open the included sample note and day.
5. [[Connection details and evidence|Connection details and evidence]] — inspect source evidence before editing.

## Try it in the help vault

Center the sample, check its Date field, then open **Settings → K-Plex → Canvas behavior → Relationship behavior → Default Date role**. With **Parent** selected, the daily note should provide upward context if Obsidian's Daily Notes target resolves. Try **Friend**, then restore Parent.

> [!important] Configured ontology wins
> If `Date` is already assigned explicitly to a role—even Hidden—the dropdown's fallback does not override it. The sample's extra Parent ontology link to its help section is separate from its Date-derived link.
