# Present and concentrate

Give the current canvas more room, then hide distractions without losing your place. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

## Follow the trail

1. [[Fullscreen and Zen mode|Fullscreen and Zen mode]] — understand the independent view controls.
2. [[Layout density and typography|Layout density and typography]] — improve readability for larger or smaller displays.
3. [[Sidecar|Sidecar]] — keep note content available while navigating.
4. [[Keyboard navigation|Keyboard navigation]] — operate without relying on hidden toolbars.
5. [[Actions and shortcuts|Actions and shortcuts]] — assign your own Zen/fullscreen gestures if desired.

## Try it in the help vault

Make this route the canvas center, pan a little, enter **Zen** with **Expand**, then use **Maximize** for fullscreen on desktop. Exit using **Shrink** and **Minimize** or unclaimed Escape presses. Notice that pan/zoom and navigation history are preserved. On mobile and tablet, try Zen only.

> [!tip] Different jobs, different modes
> **Fullscreen** covers the Obsidian workspace within its window; **Zen** hides the K-Plex toolbar and history. Both are session-only.

Continue to [[07 Customize the look and workspace|Customize the look and workspace]] or [[Choose a topic|all routes]].

## Windows controls

On Windows, the updated fullscreen/Zen layouts leave room for the operating system's native window buttons. See [[Fullscreen and Zen mode|Fullscreen and Zen mode]].
