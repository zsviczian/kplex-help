> **Your goal:** Give the current Plex more room, then hide distractions without losing your place.

## Follow the trail

1. [[05 Personalize and workspace/Fullscreen and Zen mode|Fullscreen and Zen mode]] — understand the independent view controls.
2. [[05 Personalize and workspace/Layout density and typography|Layout density and typography]] — improve readability for larger or smaller displays.
3. [[05 Personalize and workspace/Sidecar|Sidecar]] — keep note content available while navigating.
4. [[02 Navigate and explore/Keyboard navigation|Keyboard navigation]] — operate without relying on hidden toolbars.
5. [[05 Personalize and workspace/Actions and shortcuts|Actions and shortcuts]] — assign your own Zen/fullscreen gestures if desired.

## Try it in the help vault

Make this route the Plex center, pan a little, enter **Zen** with **Expand**, then use **Maximize** for fullscreen on desktop. Exit using **Shrink** and **Minimize** or unclaimed Escape presses. Notice that pan/zoom and navigation history are preserved. On mobile and tablet, try Zen only.

> [!tip] Different jobs, different modes
> **Fullscreen** covers the Obsidian workspace within its window; **Zen** hides the K-Plex toolbar and history. Both are session-only.
