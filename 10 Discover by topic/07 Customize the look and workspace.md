# Customize the look and workspace

Tune readability, imagery, companion editing and screen layout. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

You want a setup that matches how you work, including tablet or phone.

## Follow the trail

Follow the links below, or center this path in K-Plex and explore its Children.

1. **[[05 Personalize and workspace/Node styles from help tags|Node styles from help tags]]** — See how this vault visually distinguishes types of help.
2. **[[05 Personalize and workspace/Layout density and typography|Layout density and typography]]** — Adjust density, text width and wrapping.
3. **[[05 Personalize and workspace/Visibility and connector styling|Visibility and connector styling]]** — Choose which node kinds and connections to display.
4. **[[05 Personalize and workspace/Images in nodes|Images in nodes]]** — Use thumbnail and node-image properties.
5. **[[05 Personalize and workspace/Sidecar|Sidecar]]** — Read or edit the current note beside the Plex.

## Test long titles and narrow panes

With this help vault, **regular node label length defaults to 100**. Try changing density in [[05 Personalize and workspace/Layout density and typography|Layout density and typography]]; label length no longer depends on density. On a narrow pane, expand the layout panel, adjust a slider and check that the control stays accessible.

The separate [[05 Personalize and workspace/Visibility and connector styling|cross-links switch]] is currently view-local; it must be turned off manually to hide peripheral connections.
