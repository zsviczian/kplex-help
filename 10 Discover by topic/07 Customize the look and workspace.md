# Customize the look and workspace

Tune readability, imagery, companion editing and screen layout. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

You want a setup that matches how you work, including tablet or phone.

## Test long titles and narrow panes

With this help vault, **regular node label length defaults to 100**. Try changing density in [[Layout density and typography|Layout density and typography]]; label length no longer depends on density. On a narrow pane, expand the layout panel, adjust a slider and check that the control stays accessible.

The separate [[Visibility and connector styling|cross-links switch]] is currently view-local; it must be turned off manually to hide peripheral connections.
