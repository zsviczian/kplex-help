# First steps with K-Plex

Get oriented in the canvas and make your first useful navigation. Follow this short path, then try it with your own notes.

You have just installed K-Plex or opened this help vault for the first time.

## Follow the trail

Follow the links below, or center this path in K-Plex and explore its Children.

1. **[[First 5 minutes|First 5 minutes]]** — Open K-Plex, find the help home, and try the basic gestures.
2. **[[What K-Plex is|What K-Plex is]]** — Understand why the graph stays centered on one item.
3. **[[Relationship roles|Relationship roles]]** — Read the meaning of each direction before navigating.
4. **[[Navigating the Plex|Navigating the canvas]]** — Move between notes and return to the prior view.
5. **[[Vault Search vs Find in this Plex|Vault Search vs Find in this canvas]]** — Practice global navigation versus locating something on-screen.

## Try it in your own vault

Center **K-Plex Help**, move to a Child topic, then return. Try **Alt/Option+/** (or your customized shortcut) to find another topic and **Ctrl/Cmd+F** to locate a visible node.

## Try another small step

Focus the graph with **Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+G**, try **Alt/Option+.**, and compare Zen with the ordinary view.
