# K-Plex 0.4 releases

Follow the improvements from exporting and scripting your map to creating and organizing notes directly in it.

- [[Release 0.4.4|0.4.4 — Create in context and keep your place]]: separate creation tabs and templates, richer note types, saved-list previews, mobile layout and faster plugin initialization.
- [[Release 0.4.3|0.4.3 — Daily notes and a steadier map]]: calendar navigation, journal gap links, compact pins and indexing reliability.
- [[Release 0.4.2|0.4.2 — Create, organize and return to your work]]: folders and imports, periodic pins, relationship ownership, Facets options and indexing during Sync.
- [[Release 0.4.1|0.4.1 — Keep exploring as notes change]]: current Eager relationships and clearer reference-removal workflows.
- [[Release 0.4.0|0.4.0 — Put your map to work]]: export, automation and easier navigation.
